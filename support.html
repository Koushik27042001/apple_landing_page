Frequently Asked Questions

Are these genuine Apple products?

Yes. Every product is sourced from authorized distributors and comes with the standard manufacturer warranty, honoured at authorized service centers across India.

How does No-Cost EMI work?

No-Cost EMI splits your purchase into equal monthly instalments with the interest discounted as an upfront cash discount, so you pay the same as the sticker price. Availability depends on your bank/card issuer and is confirmed at checkout.

Can I pay Cash on Delivery?

COD is available on select pincodes for orders under a certain value. You'll see the option at checkout if your address is eligible.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive tracking details via SMS and email once your order ships. You can also message us on WhatsApp with your order ID for a live update.

What if my product arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage and we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — no questions asked.
